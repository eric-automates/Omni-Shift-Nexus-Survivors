Carry forward the earlier job-catalog expansion idea as a candidate for 16.0, not as an automatic extension of v15. ## Candidate sequence

1. **Preflight (no gameplay edits):** confirm the starting commit, active release switches, save/incident contracts, test selection, reachable-site and fallback capabilities, and existing medical/vehicle/dispatch dependencies. Record what is available versus missing.
2. **J06 Storm Search:** optional missing-person or stranded-driver call with prevalidated search area and exits, a timed hint, safe route/transport fallback, stabilizing and escort/handoff paths, partial/time-expiry outcome, debrief, continuation and save/reload acceptance. No aircraft is required.
3. **J08 Road Rescue:** consider after auditing the existing tow/vehicle mechanisms; provide safe roadside inspection, repair-or-tow choice, handoff and fallback without a real-engine repair minigame.
4. **J07 Emergency Airlift:** separate follow-up only after safe landing/hover and ground-response fallback are proven; no aircraft may be required where unavailable.
5. **J10–J14:** consider as individual slices—lawful tracker, protective detail, opt-in heist, smuggling, and mercenary. Each needs refusal and defined partial/escape/surrender outcomes, clear lawful/illegal boundaries, and no forced criminal assignment or civilian-target requirement.
6. **J15 universe profiles:** last; one selected profile at a time, with source citations for canon-specific claims and uncertain details labeled as design choices.

This order can change if the preflight exposes a dependency. No menu entry, feature flag, or stub counts as a completed career; any item excluded from the final agreed 16.0 scope must be explicitly deferred.
