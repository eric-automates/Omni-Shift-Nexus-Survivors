NEW IN v12 · YOUR OWN HERO, RIVAL SHOWDOWNS & EXECUTE
✨ Create your own hero (#80) — main menu → CREATE YOUR OWN HERO (or the top card of the roster). Free and always unlocked. Pick a name, Jedi / Sith / Grey, species (Human, Zabrak, Togruta, Twi'lek, Miraluka, Cyborg), skin, hair or hood, robes and cloak, a Force talent, and your sabers: one saber, a saberstaff or DUAL SABERS. Dual sabers are two separate weapons — each has its own hilt (10 hilts: Graflex, Jedi Knight, Ribbed Hermit, Dark Lord, Electrum, Curved Duelist, Shoto, Crossguard, Temple Guard, Scavenger), its own colour (10, including the Darksaber), its own level, level-up cards (#1 of 2 / #2 of 2) and Form stage. "Match #1" makes a mirrored pair. Choose a Fighting Form path and your hero climbs from Shii-Cho to its prime Form.
⚔ Rival Showdown — when a rival hero arrives, the crowd slowly backs off into a wide ring. One reckless grunt charges the rival and gets destroyed by its signature finisher, then it's you vs. the rival. The crowd watches and cheers and never attacks (your auto-aim, blades and squad ignore it). Beat the rival and the crowd is shaken before the brawl resumes. Titans and other rivals wait until the duel is over.
☠ EXECUTE stays on — after your first stagger the button stays on the HUD (dim when nothing is ready). Tap it, or press E, to finish the highest-ranked staggered elite in reach (rival → heavy → shield leader → trooper). Tap again during a finisher to chain straight into the next one. A red reticle marks who's next.
🧪 Debug All now plays 14 live actions (adds Execute Chain + Rival Showdown).
NEW IN v11 · LIGHTSABER FORMS & MUSIC
⚔️ Lightsaber Forms — blades no longer spin: every saber fights with real strikes (guard → wind-up → cut → follow-through) aimed at an actual enemy, chaining from foe to foe. The seven canon Forms each move differently: I Shii-Cho wide sweeps (Sarlacc Sweep when surrounded) · II Makashi one-handed lunges · III Soresu tight parries + ripostes · IV Ataru leaping strikes, flips, saber swarms · V Shien/Djem So bolts sent back at the shooter, Falling Avalanche overhead chops · VI Niman Pushing Slash & Draw Closer · VII Juyo/Vaapad erratic staccato flurries.
📈 Film-accurate progression — each saber hero starts in an early-career Form and unlocks the next as the weapon levels: Luke goes A New Hope Shii-Cho → Dagobah Ataru → Cloud City Djem So → Return of the Jedi Djem So (Lv.7, prime). Vader starts as Padawan Anakin; Maul ignites his second blade at the Duel of the Fates (Lv.3); Mace reaches Vaapad, Yoda his Senate-duel Ataru, Sidious Juyo; Grievous draws 2 sabers, then 4 (one per free hand), then the Utapau buzzsaw. The level-up card previews the next Form; the HUD shows the current one. Sabers ignite with a snap-hiss when enemies close in and clip back onto the belt when the area is clear.
🎵 20-track soundtrack — 17 new royalty-free tracks (synthwave, retrowave, darksynth, cyberpunk, electro, DnB, jungle, breakbeat, dubstep, techno, beats, orchestral, cinematic, metal) in the 🎵 player; filter by genre there. NEW IN v10 · HOW TO USE
⏪ Temporal ladder — HUD − / 1.0x / + step through 3.5x … 0.1x, then into REWIND (−0.1x … −5.0x). The last ~40 s rewind (enemies & corpses come back, bolts fly backwards); your XP, levels, weapons, relics and Dark Matter are kept. Re-killed "echo" enemies pay nothing. Press + or 1.0x to resume.
📻 Squad orders — tap the radio button (or press Q), or hold it and drag onto an order: ✋ Hold Position · 🎯 Focus Fire · ⬆ Advance · ↩ Regroup (keys 1-4). Radio beacons in the world call in a universe-themed fireteam; military heroes deploy with one.
☠ Glory kills — wounded elites (heavies, shield leaders, rivals) get STAGGERED. Walk into them or tap EXECUTE for a hero-specific finisher: heal, cooldown refund, XP.
🗺️ Galactic Expedition — the map replaces the stage list. Pick a route; the district you clear decides the next sector's faction, modifier, biome and warlord. The classic 50-stage list is one tap away.
🌍 Adaptive biomes — every universe has its own ground, props, weather and a hazard that hurts you AND enemies — lure them in.
🧪 Debug All — main menu → Proving Ground plays 14 live actions with your hero (v12 adds Execute Chain + Rival Showdown) and ticks each PASS/FAIL.
