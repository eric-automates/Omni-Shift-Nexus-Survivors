NEW IN v14.1 · VEHICLES, CONVOYS, ROOFS & A HUD THAT NEVER OVERLAPS
🚗 Every vehicle drives — cars, police cruisers, pickups, SUVs, buses, the army trucks of the roadblocks, tractors, semis, fire engines in their stations, forklifts, garage cars (the door opens) and every universe's ride. Push where you want to go (pull back = reverse) or switch to classic W/S + A/D in Options · Space handbrake · H horn · F in / out.
💥 Real crashes — vehicles are solid and heavy (a bus shoves a car). Hits crumple the body where they land; bumpers, hood, doors, mirrors, lights, glass and wheels tear off and stay on the road. Not dead on the first hit — but hit enough and it smokes, dies, burns and blows up. People, bullets, blasts, melee, sabers and the Force all count.
⛽ Fuel — the gauge ticks down with the throttle; stop next to a pump (TIBANNA FUEL, VOLT CHARGE, STABLES…) to refill. Below half a tank the minimap shows the stations, below 30 % it points to the nearest one.
📻 Your squad rides — they mount up when you get in, lean out of their windows (or stand in the bed / pop out of the sunroof) and shoot on their side. Too many? They split into nearby vehicles and follow you in a real tactical convoy: close column with speed-based intervals, catch-up speed, sectors (lead 12 · 9 / 3 · trail 6 o'clock), drive through ambushes, herringbone at halts, cross-load from disabled vehicles. H = mount up.
🚪 Doors you can see — every entrance has a step, frame, porch light and an arrow; garages and loading bays too; EXIT markers inside.
🏠 Roofs & fences — every building has a ladder / grav-lift (F). Teleporters: double-tap a building = inside, triple-tap = roof; jetpacks and leapers dive through a window or land on the roof. Fences are tall now: leapers vault them, climbers scale them, the T-800 and Vader go straight through. Privacy fences, hedges and garden walls hide what's behind them (chain-link and pickets don't) — unless your eyes are above them: a bus / truck / tractor cab, a jetpack, Hagrid's height or the top of a leap (and then you can be seen too).
🧩 HUD layout — nothing overlaps on any screen. Options → HUD LAYOUT: set the priority order (▲▼), tick Don't shrink, hide items, set the HUD size, live preview.
NEW IN v14 · NEXUS CITY — A 64 × 64 KM WORLD
🌆 Nexus City — every deploy drops you into a generated region with 55 cities and towns, interstates, suburbs, downtowns, farms, woods and lakes. Thousands of single-storey buildings — houses, gas stations, diners, banks, schools, police and fire stations, city halls, churches, motels, warehouses — and every one has a full interior you can walk into. Main menu → WORLD switches back to the classic arena.
🎭 Dressed for your hero — the same streets become a Tatooine adobe town, Coruscant durasteel, a neon megacity, Arrakeen, a Goa'uld temple city, an Imperial hive, the Old West, Middle-earth or Hogsmeade: buildings, furniture, floors, roofs, cars, trees, lamps, signs ("TIBANNA FUEL", "PROMETHIUM DEPOT", "FLOO POWDER CO.") and even the litter.
☠ Who hunts you — 48 enemy rosters: Stormtroopers for the Rebels, Jaffa, Horus Guards, Replicators & Kull for SG-1, the Bratva and the High Table for John Wick… spread through rooms, guard posts and patrols instead of swarming.
👂 Sound is real — a gunshot brings everyone who hears it; another shot re-targets them. Walls, doors and windows muffle it: an unsuppressed shot inside a house carries for hundreds of metres, a suppressed one barely leaves the yard, inside a sealed building it stays inside. Blades are almost silent. Ricochets and breaking glass make noise too — shoot a wall to pull a guard away. V = suppressor (real firearms only) · hold Shift = sneak (and hold fire until spotted).
👁 Line of sight — what is behind walls and closed doors is greyed out; move to see. Roofs hide other buildings' interiors until you walk in. Each hero has a unique sense: Jedi feel presences, the Predator sees green particle ghosts, Marines hear the motion tracker, Moody & Samus see through walls up close, Neo reads the code, Harry follows the Marauder's Map, John Wick hears footsteps…
🗺 Map & markers — minimap bottom-right ([ ] or the wheel to zoom). M opens the full map: click to drop a marker (6 colours × 8 symbols) with a note. Markers ride the minimap edge until you're close, then appear on it, then as a beacon on screen. From the main menu the map picks your drop zone.
🚗 Drive — walk up to a parked car (landspeeder, wagon, Anglia…) and press F. Supply caches hide inside buildings.
NEW IN v13 · THREAT SENSE, THE LIGHT SIDE, YODA & CREATOR v2
📡 Threat Sense meter — under the XP bar: 0-100% until the next rival or the Sector Titan (whichever comes first). At 100% it's here. It's dressed for your hero: Jedi feel a Disturbance in the Force, Sith feel the Dark Side stir, marines get a beeping Motion Tracker, Geralt's medallion hums, Harry's scar burns, bounty hunters feel the heat… The further you roam from the map centre, the faster it fills (×1.0 in the Outpost → ×2.1 at the Abyssal Core). At 90%+ an arrow on the screen edge shows where it will come from.
☯ Force Persuasion — Luke, Mace, Yoda (and your own Light-side hero who is Strong in the Force) automatically turn a few nearby enemies to their side when a crowd closes in. Turned enemies glow, get a halo and a green health bar, are healed to double health and fight for you — your shots and blades pass through them. When it wears off they lay down their arms and leave (half XP). Rivals and Titans can't be persuaded.
🟢 Yoda, Grand Master — Lv.4 Ataru leaps · Lv.5 Force Lift & Hurl (lifts up to 8 foes and throws them) · Lv.7 his Ataru bounds from foe to foe, and his ultimate WILL OF THE FORCE: he puts his lightsaber away, raises a hand, and a wave of the Force crosses the whole screen — every enemy levitates; the weak are obliterated, the rest turn to the light and fight for him for 25 s (new arrivals are still hostile). His ward is now a true Tutaminis.
✨ Hero Creator v2 — build any character: 1-3 weapons from 26 (lightsaber, saberstaff, blades, six-shooter & blasters, rifles, heavy guns, wands & staves, tech), exactly 2 skills, 1 signature gear, 1 mobility move and 1 armor — every choice has honest requirements (🔒 shows what's missing). Two of the same weapon are real twins (own level, own reload, own target). Mandalorian beskar comes with a jetpack and you really fly — over rocks, walls, water and hazards. 8 presets: Jedi Knight, Sith Lord, Twin-Gun Drifter, Force-Touched Wizard, Space Cowboy, Smuggler, Mandalorian, Jar'Kai Duelist.
NEW IN v12 · YOUR OWN HERO, RIVAL SHOWDOWNS & EXECUTE
✨ Create your own hero (#80) — main menu → CREATE YOUR OWN HERO (or the top card of the roster). Free and always unlocked. Pick a name, Jedi / Sith / Grey, species (Human, Zabrak, Togruta, Twi'lek, Miraluka, Cyborg), skin, hair or hood, robes and cloak, a Force talent, and your sabers: one saber, a saberstaff or DUAL SABERS. Dual sabers are two separate weapons — each has its own hilt (10 hilts: Graflex, Jedi Knight, Ribbed Hermit, Dark Lord, Electrum, Curved Duelist, Shoto, Crossguard, Temple Guard, Scavenger), its own colour (10, including the Darksaber), its own level, level-up cards (#1 of 2 / #2 of 2) and Form stage. "Match #1" makes a mirrored pair. Choose a Fighting Form path and your hero climbs from Shii-Cho to its prime Form.
⚔ Rival Showdown — when a rival hero arrives, the crowd slowly backs off into a wide ring. One reckless grunt charges the rival and gets destroyed by its signature finisher, then it's you vs. the rival. The crowd watches and cheers and never attacks (your auto-aim, blades and squad ignore it). Beat the rival and the crowd is shaken before the brawl resumes. Titans and other rivals wait until the duel is over.
☠ EXECUTE stays on — after your first stagger the button stays on the HUD (dim when nothing is ready). Tap it, or press E, to finish the highest-ranked staggered elite in reach (rival → heavy → shield leader → trooper). Tap again during a finisher to chain straight into the next one. A red reticle marks who's next.

NEW IN v10 · HOW TO USE
⏪ Temporal ladder — HUD − / 1.0x / + step through 3.5x … 0.1x, then into REWIND (−0.1x … −5.0x). The last ~40 s rewind (enemies & corpses come back, bolts fly backwards); your XP, levels, weapons, relics and Dark Matter are kept. Re-killed "echo" enemies pay nothing. Press + or 1.0x to resume.
📻 Squad orders — tap the radio button (or press Q), or hold it and drag onto an order: ✋ Hold Position · 🎯 Focus Fire · ⬆ Advance · ↩ Regroup (keys 1-4). Radio beacons in the world call in a universe-themed fireteam; military heroes deploy with one.
☠ Glory kills — wounded elites (heavies, shield leaders, rivals) get STAGGERED. Walk into them or tap EXECUTE for a hero-specific finisher: heal, cooldown refund, XP.
🗺️ Galactic Expedition — the map replaces the stage list. Pick a route; the district you clear decides the next sector's faction, modifier, biome and warlord. The classic 50-stage list is one tap away.
🌍 Adaptive biomes — every universe has its own ground, props, weather and a hazard that hurts you AND enemies — lure them in.
🧪 Debug All — main menu → Proving Ground plays 14 live actions with your hero (v12 adds Execute Chain + Rival Showdown) and ticks each PASS/FAIL.
