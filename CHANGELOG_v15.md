## Work started
- Local Dispatch now offers an optional three-stop medical distribution shift in economy mode. Each stop checks a pickup, route choice and real clinic entrance; the route may include an abstract insulated relay, and a damageable civilian clinic contact accepts the case. Each successful stop saves its receipt, wallet payment and shared-town medicine change together; an early end keeps completed results. The next route prefers a different reachable clinic but can return to the same one. The player can set down and recover the case, find a replacement waypoint, or use a single emergency canister on a nearby stopped low-fuel vehicle they drove. Vehicle fuel remains run-local. A lost clinic contact prevents that stop's delivery; a living calm contact who cannot reach the entrance can accept an off-site partial handoff, without a medicine-stock effect. The shift has no persistent assigned vehicle or dynamic road closure; it is not the full requested courier career. Routes are validated on foot, not guaranteed traversable around later moving obstacles. Movement and injury checkpoint limitations remain.
- A separate optional medical delivery now includes a damageable civilian clinic contact. They wait at the clinic entrance or walk in from an off-screen, route-checked position; approach them on foot for a real acknowledgment. Injury/fleeing, fatal loss and failed saves cannot falsely authorize delivery; an explicitly abstract partial handoff remains available. The stock effect is shared town medicine, not per-clinic simulation, and this is not a doctor, ambulance, crew, or complete courier career. Older abstract medical calls remain available.
- The witness escort can optionally request an unarmed civilian support contact who walks to the meeting point and can flee or be hurt. A supported handoff requires both living, calm people to actually meet; the earlier unguarded escort remains available and pays the same. The worker is not police, a guard, a medic or a guaranteed safe destination. Spawn/reload waits for an off-screen clear site; injury and movement saves remain subject to storage and interruption limits. Active mission contacts no longer randomly convert into unrelated street enemies.
- An injured, calm witness in the optional investigation can receive one limited first-aid action when the player is nearby on foot. The health change and once-only flag are saved together; it cannot revive a lost witness. This is a small aid interaction, not simulated medical equipment or a responder.
- In the optional missing-witness lead, the witness can describe a person who pressured them. Choose to escort immediately or follow a route-checked lead to a distinct, damageable civilian and ask for their account; they deny the allegation, and the debrief records that the accounts are unverified rather than declaring guilt or an arrest. The person can flee or die, their health and movement are checkpointed with the same storage/interruption limits as the witness, and you can abandon the lead. No confrontation, weapon, arrest, responder or full detective career is simulated.
- Convoy halt parking now tries forward positions along the selected shoulder only when sampled footprints and leader spacing are clear. It waits and retries when no candidate is clear, and uses sampled early braking around parking cars and nearby people while retaining ordinary vehicle physics. This helps some physical arrivals, but is not continuous swept-path certification: followers can still stop short or remain blocked, and pre-existing contacts can persist.
- Witness Lead in economy/apocalypse can now continue from a civilian contact to a separate missing witness. On foot, approach and escort the damageable person to an unguarded meeting point; they follow using collision-aware walking, stop if you drive or fly, flee danger, and a saved fatal loss prevents a false success. A marker guides you back if you leave them behind; the witness returns to ordinary town behavior after the result. No suspect, responder, crew or complete career is simulated, and this is not a guarded handoff. Movement and injury checkpoints have the same interruption/storage limits described below.
- New Witness Lead calls in economy and apocalypse scenarios now include a damageable civilian contact at the follow-up. Approach on foot and file their limited statement or hand the unresolved lead off; alarm or injury can interrupt the interview, and a successfully saved fatal loss prevents filing it. The contact is not the missing person and identifies no suspect. Nonfatal injuries are checkpointed immediately; a moving contact’s position is checkpointed every three simulated seconds when it changes, and restored at the saved location only if it remains walkable. Recent movement can be lost on interruption, and a failed storage write cannot promise an injury or death survives reload. Survivors-mode and older saved calls remain abstract. This is a small lead, not the complete detective career.
- Medical courier rerouting can look for additional walkable sites alongside the same clinic door when the original outward positions are unavailable. A candidate must remain close to the door, clear its frontage and have a validated on-foot route; if none works, dispatch still fails safely.
- Local Dispatch offers an optional short **Witness Lead** investigation: review a report, inspect a lead, then survey a follow-up or hand it off unresolved. Its three sites are checked on foot, checkpoints are saved, and blocked sites can be replanned. The original short survey branch does not locate the person; the later optional escort described above adds a witness but no suspect or responder, and the career remains incomplete.
- A courier handoff now rechecks the current incident and whether its case is carried. A stale dispatch callback or another handoff caller cannot close and pay an incident after the case is set down; recover the case first. Existing abstract handoff payouts and save formats are unchanged.
- Some existing generated farms placed a hay bale inside a parked tractor, leaving it unable to move. When an unmoved tractor is entered or put in a convoy, overlapping bales can now be moved to checked nearby open ground outside the vehicle and its immediate front/rear clearance. Other obstacles are not removed; if no safe spot exists, the bale stays. The move is runtime-only, limited to 128 bales per run, and does not alter the original seeded layout or saved locations. Other parked-vehicle escape issues remain.
- A working police cruiser on an active mapped traffic network now has an optional stationary **RADAR** observation tool. Choose front, rear or both; it identifies and marks the nearest moving same-link vehicle in a narrow beam with line of sight, displays its real-equivalent speed, mapped speed limit, distance and motion, and refreshes while open. It does not ticket, stop or classify anyone, and does not yet provide the planned police career or player-led stop. It can miss vehicles on other mapped links or behind buildings; it does not model occlusion by other cars, and no reading is not an all-clear. Stop the cruiser to scan.
- Courier cases can now be visibly carried or voluntarily set down on open outdoor ground. The case location is saved with the incident, can be recovered via the normal touch action after reload, and must be recovered before progressing or requesting a handoff. It does not fall automatically or simulate damage or inventory weight; withdrawing still exits the call. Older active incidents without a stored case location remain carried.
- Convoy catch-up no longer relocates a vehicle unless its whole footprint is outside both the last rendered and current calculated views at both endpoints, and the destination remains clear. Unknown camera state waits. A follower behind its slot can now drive forward toward the halted column instead of stopping far behind; obstacle and leader-distance checks still apply. Other difficult traffic and obstruction cases remain under investigation.
- Optional courier calls now let a player request a different reachable objective site if the current one is blocked. The current stage and fixed payout stay the same; priority-call branches still require reachable later routes and failed storage writes roll back. Local dispatch also shows the last three recorded courier outcomes and pay after reload. These calls and handoffs remain abstract, not a full career or simulated responders.
- When a nearby car or kiosk masks a person, the single touch-action button now offers a choice instead of hiding TALK or making the car inaccessible. The chooser pauses the simulation, validates targets, supports touch and keyboard, and closes safely on reset; a stale prompt also cannot activate a different car, kiosk or person. Tested in emulation, not on a physical phone.
- The HUD layout now hides a lower-priority optional item once it reaches minimum size before shrinking a higher-priority control. This prevents a late telemetry pill from shifting the portrait minimap buttons beneath a tap in the reproduced startup case; HUD priority and “Don’t shrink” preferences still apply.
- Added an optional priority courier call alongside the existing short delivery: collect and assess a case, then choose direct delivery or an insulated relay and delivery, with distinct fixed payouts and durable checkpoints. Routes are checked on foot before an offer; handoff is abstract and there is no temperature simulation, NPC responder or complete courier career.
- Fatal direct ballistic hits and later fatal bleeding from those wounds on law officers now use the same launch-position identification check as civilian direct-shot hits: a witness seeing the incident but not the launch position does not automatically identify the player. Launch geometry survives rewind; newer non-ballistic injuries and reused slots clear stale evidence. This is geometric at the time of death, not real witness memory; explosions and unconverted paths retain their earlier limitations. Rewind history is about 35.2 seconds under the existing 30 MiB cap (previously 36.3 seconds before the extra evidence).
- Custom-hero creation now offers an optional **Simulated World Job** preference: none (default), or supply courier. The latter opens the existing optional dispatch dialogue on city deployment if it is safe to do so; it does not accept a job or replace equipment. Close it or find work later through the city map. This is not a full career or a complete shift.
- Traffic police no longer accumulate an unseen violation and then charge it when a later violation is witnessed. During a pursuit, a real officer or occupied cruiser must see a new violation before it is added to the current stop; an empty parked cruiser cannot see. If a sight check fails, police do not assume visibility. This is limited traffic-offense evidence, not a complete witness-memory or officer-identity system.
- Routine traffic-stop officers now use collision-aware pathfinding for their approach. If genuinely stuck for twelve seconds, the stop is canceled without a ticket or added heat, and an existing officer returns to the cruiser rather than walking through a wall. Actual obstacle layouts and on-screen recovery remain limited by the existing pathfinder.
- Added one optional short local supply dispatch, accessible from the in-run city map: collect, deliver, decline or withdraw, or request an explicitly abstract partial handoff after pickup. Sites are validated for outdoor clearance and on-foot reachability; checkpoints and fixed outcomes persist with once-only pay. This is a small repeatable variation, not the complete courier career or its planned multi-stage mission. No dispatch NPC, weight or local service effect is simulated yet; later work added the recoverable case noted above.
- Added an internal, bounded career checkpoint/transaction foundation. The later optional supply dispatch uses this foundation; full careers and scenarios are not complete. Progress and wallet updates are saved in one transaction with rollback on failed writes and once-only authored payouts.
- Persuaded allies’ bolts and melee, enemy attacks against persuaded allies, and seated squad melee now preserve broad actor categories instead of defaulting to player blame. These are not yet actor-ID or command-responsibility rules.
- Browser storage write failures show a touch-accessible warning and retry button; progress and options are tracked separately. Unreadable or invalid saved data is protected from automatic overwrites; readable stored data can be deliberately restored, and unreadable raw data can be downloaded for recovery. Restoring stored data replaces unsaved session changes. Existing save keys and formats are unchanged. This does not yet offer automatic repair/import or new career persistence.
- Known player/enemy/squad/unknown projectile and rocket causes now reach the economy officer-death handler, including delayed bleeding and reflected shots; known non-player causes do not add player heat on those tested paths. Shot launch coordinates and bleeding source survive rewind. Known squad damage is not yet tied to individual orders, and unconverted legacy kill paths still use legacy attribution. The added rewind records reduce the measured history from about 38.3 to 36.3 seconds under the same 30 MiB cap.

Flying and hovering use a directional held pose rather than a running leg cycle; brooms and parachutes stay with the hero. Above 60 m the game enters ground-out-of-range travel: ground-plane shots and attacks cannot hit the flyer, and the hero's new ground-targeted attacks wait until descent (without spending their cooldowns). Already-launched ground attacks and squad activity continue. Crashes and non-combat needs still matter.
- Jetpack and aircraft use cached aerial ground rendering earlier when the camera is sufficiently zoomed out and the player is safely above the surface. The detailed city returns near roofs and during descent. This changes rendering, not ground simulation or collision; the normal hero/craft stays visible until the original far-view conditions. Some map streaming/tile-generation stutters remain possible.

Existing saved sizes and map/drop keys are preserved; an old saved default is indistinguishable from a chosen size and is not silently changed.
- Traffic police no longer restart a stop while one is active. During an active nearby fight the officer gets out and waits instead of forcing a paused ticket conversation. Walking away from a stop escalates once into evasion; an officer already outside does not teleport into the cruiser.
- Added an optional road-vehicle CRUISE speed hold. It does not steer or avoid obstacles; steering and braking remain the player's responsibility. Pulling back, handbrake, exiting, pausing, opening the map, running out of fuel, a major impact, or a disabled/burning vehicle turns it off.

- The basic infantry follow behavior now uses stable travel-oriented slots rather than circling the player, with a limited catch-up speed and a brief regroup response. Full road-aware pathfinding, cornering and universe-specific doctrine are not done.
- Projectiles record broad player/hostile/squad origin; hostile and squad rounds are no longer automatically recorded as the player's shots when they hit civilians. Context-aware squad responsibility, additional damage sources and warning/assist settings are not done.

## Not complete

### Progress backup (incremental, not a complete v15 release)
- Options now offers an explicit progress-only JSON download, import, and one local pre-import recovery copy. Import requires confirmation, preserves the prior raw progress before replacing it, rejects structurally invalid/oversized input and aborts during an active run or on a storage failure. Older/unknown progress fields are retained; unreadable progress cannot be exported as default data. Options and an active run are not included. The recovery slot replaces any earlier pre-import copy; close other game tabs first. This is not a cloud backup, a complete world save, a full semantic validator, or a crash-proof multi-key transaction.

### Vehicle glass correction
- A projectile selected as hitting a 2.5D windshield no longer damages that same pane a second time during the nearby-part pass. A two-point windshield now cracks on one such hit and breaks on the second; the low/high random selection branches remain. Vehicle regression coverage uses a reproducible seed and also verifies that a shot can reach an occupant.

### Radioed cruiser arrival
- A radioed patrol car now waits for an eligible trail position outside both the last rendered and current calculated camera views, including a safety margin for the vehicle's footprint. If no view or off-screen trail position is available, it retries and eventually cancels the encounter without a visible pop-in. This applies only to this arrival path; it is not a complete police escalation or pursuit overhaul.

### Off-screen police catch-up
- An existing pursuing cruiser can catch up along the recorded trail only when its entire body is outside both the last rendered and current camera view at both ends. No catch-up occurs with an unavailable view. This does not make the pursuit collision-aware or complete police escalation.

### In development — convoy obstruction controls
- Followers now brake rather than creep into a close obstruction, and resume when the path opens.
- After waiting at a persistent blockage, a follower can briefly reverse straight only while a conservative rear-footprint check remains clear. Boxed-in vehicles wait and retry. Existing stuck reversals also check the rear; their forced forward phase is canceled at an immediate obstacle. Collision physics remain active.
- These are incremental controls, not a guarantee of route recovery: an obstruction may still cause repeated back-and-forth, and natural-traffic convoy scenarios remain under investigation.
- At a halt, followers prefer alternating shoulders but can try the opposite shoulder when the preferred parking target is obstructed; candidate footprints are checked against terrain and live vehicles. If both are blocked, the vehicle holds its column position.

### In development — civilian vehicle collisions
- Physical vehicles now interact with outdoor townsfolk using the vehicle footprint and relative impact speed. Low-speed contact pushes; higher-speed impacts cause injury, with a short per-vehicle repeat-hit guard. Indoor people and airborne vehicles are excluded.
- The player is recorded as the cause only for the vehicle they actually drive; convoy drivers are recorded as squad and other drivers as unknown. The existing civilian-consequences switch continues to control player-facing crime consequences, not injury itself. This does not complete squad-order attribution or the wider witness model.

### In development — optional auto-fire safety assist
- In options, Auto-fire Safety Assist can hold an individual straight auto-fired projectile when its actual firing lane overlaps a locally simulated, visible civilian. Neo's two independent Uzi lanes and projectile spreads are checked separately. It is off by default and independent of warnings, squad discipline and civilian-consequence settings.
- A held shot displays HOLD FIRE · CIVILIAN; the next clear shot resumes. A fully held volley does not consume an economy round or a revolver cylinder shot. Touch options are at least 44 pixels tall for coarse pointers.
- This is a limited assist, not an all-clear: it does not model blast areas, ricochets, hidden/unsimulated people, manual utilities, or non-straight projectile effects. The existing off-screen warning remains a separate broader proximity caution.

## In progress — medical courier and economy stock (15.0.28)

- Adds an optional, short economy-mode courier call to an actual nearby generated clinic or hospital entrance. Full completion pays 140 and adds up to six units to shared town medicine stock, capped at four times reference stock; it does not create an NPC recipient or per-clinic inventory. An explicitly abstract handoff pays 35 without restocking.
- The entrance is revalidated against its recorded building; an alternate delivery objective remains at the same building. Case recovery, bounded history, save rollback, and objective markers reuse the existing courier system. A call is unavailable when no reachable medical entrance is nearby.
- A HUD refresh error cannot invalidate an already durable job/wallet checkpoint. This is not the completed planned courier career; further missions, characters, consequences and end-to-end acceptance remain.

## In progress — medical call availability and checkpoint safety (15.0.29)

- If the first reachable pickup does not connect to a nearby medical site, dispatch checks up to three more deterministic pickup candidates, each requiring two valid foot routes. No unreachable site is substituted; the interface suggests moving closer when the bounded search finds no suitable pair.
- Completion rechecks the current saved objective and case state, so a stale pre-reroute or pre-drop snapshot cannot award a delivery.
- Verified actual clinic calls in generated Nexus and offline Indianapolis, including old and full real-map scales. This remains a short optional call rather than the planned complete career; it does not guarantee that every location has a reachable medical call.

## In progress — civilian reactions and straight spell safety (15.0.30)

- Civilian hit reactions now flee from an estimated incoming projectile direction or actual nearby impact vehicle, instead of automatically fleeing from the hero after hostile or squad fire. Unknown impacts fall back to movement facing; danger direction is not used as proof of attacker identity.
- The optional known-civilian auto-fire assist also checks straight style-5 magic/chain projectiles. It does not predict blasts, ricochets, hidden civilians, area attacks or manual utilities, and remains off by default.
- Direct straight style-5 hits use the same launch-position witness-geometry check as existing straight projectiles, including delayed officer injury and rewind. This remains a geometric approximation rather than historical witness memory or full actor-level attribution.

## In progress — safe interruption controls (15.0.31)

- If the game loses focus, release held keys, joystick, vehicle handbrake and cruise, flight and ki buttons, pending long-press actions, and map drag. A hidden tab or pagehide pauses an actively running game with the existing touch-accessible resume screen; already-paused overlays are preserved. The game does not silently resume when the tab returns.
- Pause and quit controls use 44 px minimum height. No new save write is performed during interruption; live run state still cannot be recovered after a browser/process termination, and mobile lifecycle events are not guaranteed.

## In progress — courier assistant and clinic approach (15.0.44)

- During a medical-distribution delivery, optionally request an unarmed civilian assistant. They approach from a route-checked off-screen location, can accept or return the case only when calm and physically met, and carry it on foot toward the real clinic contact. They can flee, be injured or killed; they do not enter vehicles. Delivery still requires the player, carrier and living calm recipient to meet at the clinic, and saves the same receipt, pay and town stock atomically. The assistant can be used once per stop; if an approach cannot be found, deliver yourself.
- If an assistant is killed while carrying the case, it is recoverable where they fell if the actual location is safe for a case. If it is obstructed, end the shift early; no payout is invented. The assistant's last checkpoint and health are saved periodically, so movement since the last successful checkpoint can be lost on crash or storage failure. A restored assistant appears only outside the current and last view.
- Clinic contacts now continue walking normally until closer to their entrance and with body-clear line of sight, rather than stopping at 25 pixels even when the delivery gate remains blocked. This improves sampled cases, not a guarantee against navigation stalls or moving obstacles.
- This remains a short, partial courier shift: no assigned van, authored road closure or cargo threat, full career progression, or validated 7–12 minute arc. Physical Android has not been tested.

## In progress — optional work vehicle (15.0.45)

- During a medical distribution shift the player can ask for one actual, drivable work vehicle per run in supported settings. A bounded search checks off-screen road-edge space, its body, nearby actors, vehicles, static obstacles, a short sampled pull-out and an on-foot approach; if no candidate is available, dispatch offers no vehicle and the player may continue without it. Later moving traffic and obstacles can still block or strike it. The assistant remains on foot.
- The amber minimap square marks the actual vehicle while it exists; normal vehicle fuel, damage, driving and collisions apply. Selected city-theme vehicle types are used; settings without an appropriate civilian profile offer no work vehicle.
- The issuance is saved once per shift, but the existing vehicle simulation is **run-local**. After a run reset or browser reload the vehicle is not restored or automatically replaced, including when it was damaged; the player may continue with another vehicle or on foot. This is not a persistent fleet, certified route or the completed courier career.
