# CHRONO-LATTICE RATCHET LEDGER — v15.0 baseline candidate, not a final release

Under the CHRONO-LATTICE RATCHET LEDGER, 15.0.1–15.0.8 stabilize working features, `v15.0.99-OMEGA` is the planned verification and packaging breakpoint, and shelved feature work follows in 15.1+. This is a roadmap, not evidence of release readiness. The attached ledger lives at `CHRONO_LATTICE_RATCHET_LEDGER_v15.md`.

This profile preserves the older game and saved preferences while exposing tested parts of the new careers. Disabled does not mean finished. Unknown or unmapped contracts are held rather than newly offered unless a QA fixture explicitly enables `window.V15_INTERNAL_TEST_MODE === true`; that flag is not a security boundary. Build-time switches in `js/releaseprofile.js` control new offers and temporarily hold an in-progress saved job unchanged when its switch is off. The player can review it on the map and resume in the same world if that switch is restored. Unsaved actor movement before the last successful checkpoint cannot be guaranteed. The creator's **Simulated World Job** preference and the map provide optional access; neither automatically accepts a job.

## Available optional calls

- **Supply:** short collection/delivery with abstract early handoff.
- **Priority delivery:** direct or optional insulated relay.
- **Medical delivery:** economy-mode clinic delivery; an optional separate variant has a damageable, fleeing civilian clinic contact. The clinic restock is capped and paid once.
- **Medical distribution shift:** three connected clinic deliveries with optional relay, an unarmed civilian assistant walking on foot, and an optional run-local work vehicle and one-use fuel canister. The assistant cannot board vehicles. The loan vehicle and fuel changes are not restored after restart, although issuance and any used emergency canister are saved; the active-shift dispatch panel states this. A vehicle request may safely fail if no site is available. This is not the full courier career or a guaranteed 7–12-minute mission.
- **Witness lead:** a small investigation with route-checked sites. In a town with active NPCs an ordinary damageable civilian contact can be interviewed, followed by an optional missing-witness search and on-foot escort to an **unguarded** meeting point. An optional unarmed civilian support worker and first aid are available where the conditions permit; a person of interest is not treated as proven guilty. In other modes the follow-up is abstract. This is not a police response, guarded handoff, arrest, complete detective career, or full responder simulation.

The witness/contact, interview and support hydration paths permit an absent checkpoint for an unencountered person, but reject a present null, incomplete or invalid person snapshot without synthesizing health or overwriting the save. An escort without a saved witness checkpoint also fails closed. The unresolved handoff remains available; a missing off-screen actor is not proof of health. This guard does not establish the cause of the earlier disappearance or Python fixture failure.

The witness call has passed 39 focused QA-only cases, a five-leg Indianapolis emulated-touch/simulated-walking flow in both orientations, and an injured-escort save hold/resume fixture. A forced A/B reproduced a possible identity-replacement mechanism in the old random crime selector while the existing guard prevented it; **the original historical disappearance/C66 failure was not traced**, so the old cause remains unknown. The first hold/resume fixture failed without useful diagnostics; a version positioning the player at the saved witness passed. These checks do not establish universal routing or physical Android behavior.

## Flight and touch-control boundary

The 15.0.4 landscape touch strip clamps its five flight controls within tested short viewports without moving the primary action/exit or self-flight toggle. Very short landscape screens use a compact row. This is not a universal no-overlap guarantee, a full adaptive/manual HUD redesign, or physical Android verification; device cutouts and unusual viewport dimensions remain unverified. Above the existing 60 m boundary the game separates ground-plane combat rather than simulating full 3D air combat.

## Law, radar and civilian safety boundary (15.0.5 scoped checkpoint)

A narrow touch fix for the radar HUD button resolves a short touch on release and suppresses the matching synthesized click, while leaving mouse/keyboard activation and canceled or dragged touches distinct. This is not a general HUD redesign or physical-device sign-off.

The working cruiser radar is observation-only and requires a stopped, working police vehicle on a mapped road; its narrow beam and same-road/visibility checks can miss vehicles and do not initiate tickets or traffic stops. Traffic citations depend on observed offenses; an officer stuck on a routine approach abandons the stop after more than 12 seconds without a ticket. Radioed-unit spawning checks both rendered and current camera views; physical-device behavior and every road or pursuit remain unverified.

An installed-source civilian-reaction check (H45) failed once without a projectile hit; subsequent instrumented runs passed, but the original cause remains unconfirmed. The scoped 15.0.5 checkpoint is locked with that historical uncertainty explicitly retained. Controlled A/B tests demonstrate that a slow simulation can exceed the fixture’s fixed 90-step wait and a vehicle can intercept the projectile, but the original result did not capture enough information to identify its cause. The QA fixture now records relevant projectile, timing and vehicle details without changing its pass criterion. A later QA-only adjustment measures waiting in simulation time, rejects already-occupied live-vehicle lanes, and temporarily disables ambient traffic generation for the direct-hit fixture; it restores the user setting and retains the hit, heat and flee assertions. A vehicle inserted after selection remains a recorded failure, and a separate real-map traffic fixture checks projectile interception. A newer failed run captured vehicle interception after lane selection; the older failure is still unexplained.

The opt-in Auto-fire Safety Assist covers supported automatic straight shots toward known civilians, not blasts, all attack types, or unseen people. Civilian injury consequences, civilian warning and squad fire discipline are independent options. Tested direct ballistic/straight-spell evidence, delayed bleeding sources and projectile launch coordinates survive relevant rewind paths. Known non-player attacks do not create player heat in the tested paths, but legacy/unconverted causes and actor-specific command responsibility remain limitations. Persistent long-term witness memory and player-operated traffic stops, tickets, arrests, transport and tow trucks are deferred to 15.3.x.

## Player-car squad boundary (15.0.6 scoped checkpoint)

With overflow convoy disabled, eligible squad members can board the player's vehicle; those without a compatible seat hold position with a map marker. Horn and player re-entry refresh the shared hold marker; a nearby on-foot return or a newly available seat releases the affected members. These are sampled behaviors, not a guarantee of every terrain, combat or pathing situation. Player-entry overlap safeguards remain for tractor hay and army-truck bus stops; if relocation cannot be safely found, the prop remains. Warehouse forklift placement prefers an open strip but may retain a fallback when none exists. AI overflow convoy and herringbone remain deferred, not fixed.

## Save, backup and map-scale boundary (15.0.7 scoped checkpoint)

If stored progress or options cannot be read, saving that data is blocked and the warning offers a raw-data download when bytes are available. Failed storage writes are reported and can be retried after storage is available. Explicit progress backup exports JSON; importing replaces progress after confirmation, retains one prior local raw progress value, and leaves options and active-run state out. Restoring the pre-import copy replaces current progress. Download a separate backup first, close other game tabs, and do not clear site data while a warning is unresolved. These mechanisms cannot guarantee recovery from device loss, browser eviction or concurrent writes and do not constitute cloud backup or an atomic multi-key transaction.

Real-map DEFAULT is the internal half-scale baseline, with relative ±25% options. Existing 1:3, 1:2 and full-size world/drop identities remain distinct; labels do not rename old keys. These are sampled browser checks, not a procedural-world coordinate migration or comprehensive legacy-save/device verification. Generated-world road and building rescaling stays deferred.

## Baseline gate boundary (15.0.8 scoped checkpoint)

The separate `--profile=v15.0-omega` QA gate does not replace the default, stricter full-final gate. It requires a source-matched serial ten-pack, every non-deferred/non-exception test to pass, the exact disabled-feature ledger, and documented local-asset results. It does not turn S10 or R20 into passes or establish remote asset availability. A09, A10, F04 and the convoy-specific A11, A12 and D01 are deferred; the latter three cover the disabled convoy or its herringbone workload. Full careers are deferred while their individually enabled partial calls remain subject to their tests.

At the Omega breakpoint, the external QA runner/stamper and gate must pass before the separately verified matching staging overlay can be packaged. The `v15.0-omega` package profile requires both the baseline report and stage-integrity record. This checkpoint did not run the full ten-pack or refresh/package the stage; current staging is stale. Neither the gate nor local ZIP verification proves omitted remote assets, a merged site or physical device behavior.

See the local `V15_REVISED_PLAN.md` and `V15_STABLE_TASK_TRACKER.md` for the larger scope and current estimates.

The optional work-vehicle request now permits a retry if construction fails before any vehicle is registered and the cancellation saves successfully. If registration is ambiguous or cancellation cannot be saved, it retains the issuance to avoid duplicates. Vehicle/fuel still do not resume after a restart. Failure recovery has been fault-injected in Chromium; it is not proof that every browser storage or vehicle failure is handled.
