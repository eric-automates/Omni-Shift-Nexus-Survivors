**Snapshot date:** 2026-10-27. The 15.6 series is locked at 92%; committed-tree evidence is recorded in `qa/CHRONO_1567_15.6-SERIES-LOCKED_REPORT.md`. This scope document is not deployment approval.

Under the CHRONO-LATTICE RATCHET LEDGER, 15.0.1–15.0.8 stabilize working features, `v15.0.99-OMEGA` is the planned verification and packaging breakpoint, and shelved feature work follows in 15.1+. This is a roadmap, not evidence of release readiness. The attached ledger lives at `CHRONO_LATTICE_RATCHET_LEDGER_v15.md`.

This profile preserves the older game and saved preferences while exposing tested parts of the new careers. Disabled does not mean finished. Unknown or unmapped contracts are held rather than newly offered unless a QA fixture explicitly enables `window.V15_INTERNAL_TEST_MODE === true`; that flag is not a security boundary. Build-time switches in `js/releaseprofile.js` control new offers and temporarily hold an in-progress saved job unchanged when its switch is off. The player can review it on the map and resume in the same world if that switch is restored. Unsaved actor movement before the last successful checkpoint cannot be guaranteed. The creator's **Simulated World Job** preference and the map provide optional access; neither automatically accepts a job.

## Available optional calls

- **Supply:** short collection/delivery with abstract early handoff.
- **Priority delivery:** direct or optional insulated relay.
- **Medical delivery:** economy-mode clinic delivery; an optional separate variant has a damageable, fleeing civilian clinic contact. The clinic restock is capped and paid once.
- **Medical distribution shift:** three connected clinic deliveries with optional relay, an unarmed civilian assistant who can also board the loan vehicle as a passenger, and an optional run-local work vehicle and one-use fuel canister. The issued loan vehicle's position, damage and fuel restore across reloads; a canister used mid-run is consumed honestly and a vanished vehicle falls back to on-foot with the walk saved. A vehicle request may safely fail if no site is available. The full courier career now spans five stages derived from durably completed shifts (a withdrawal never counts, corrupt counts fail closed) with a rank pay bonus, planning-time road-closure rerouting that re-routes only the leg it blocks and replans honestly around a pinned closure, and a rookie-to-lead flagship arc measured at 7.2–11.6 minutes under the QA pacing model (a 4.3-minute walk-only floor over the committed A* geometry; the optional loan vehicle, traffic and hazards can move real time outside that band — no in-game timer enforces any duration). Closures are dispatch routing decisions on the pedestrian grid: they are not physical barricades, render or traffic behavior is unchanged, and a shift is never blocked by its own rerouter or its own pacing.
- **Witness lead (detective career):** an investigation with route-checked sites. In a town with active NPCs an ordinary damageable civilian contact can be interviewed, followed by an optional missing-witness search and on-foot escort to a meeting point. An optional unarmed civilian support worker and first aid are available where the conditions permit. While a described person's account is open, they can be **confronted once** per case — their reaction (denial, silence, or walking off) is a deterministic, saved **observation, never proof or a finding of guilt**. At the meeting point, **pedestrian responders can be called** for an optional **guarded handoff**: two damageable, unarmed walkers reach the witness on foot and the case closes with the witness in their presence. The five-stage detective career (Scene clerk → Homicide consultant) counts only filed cases — unresolved handoffs and withdrawals never count, corrupt counts fail closed — and the stage bonus pays out inside the same single finish transaction that records the history, with the panel deriving its promised amounts from the same math. That witness-rescue call itself does not simulate cruiser traffic or custody; responders are on foot because the simulation has no dispatch traffic system; a hurt or scattered responder only makes the guarded ending unavailable; and the meeting point otherwise remains fully unguarded. In other modes the follow-up stays abstract.

The witness/contact, interview and support hydration paths permit an absent checkpoint for an unencountered person, but reject a present null, incomplete or invalid person snapshot without synthesizing health or overwriting the save. An escort without a saved witness checkpoint also fails closed. The unresolved handoff remains available; a missing off-screen actor is not proof of health. This guard does not establish the cause of the earlier disappearance or Python fixture failure.

Since 15.3.1 the same promise holds **inside** a session: every mission actor (contact, witness, person of interest, support worker, called responder, clinic contact) is pinned to a durable person record. Walking out of streaming range checkpoints their live state — health, stun, flight, escort flags, standing posture and position — onto the person; walking back restores that same person with that state, and proximity gates hydrate the record before judging absence. Re-registering a known id refreshes role fields but can never replace the human being, and the night crime-selector pass cannot take a pinned person. Ordinary citizens are untouched. This makes the old C66 class of "witness silently returns at full health / vanishes until reload" structurally impossible in-session. Long-term, cross-case witness memory is a separate J02 feature shipped at 15.6.6.

The witness call has passed 39 focused QA-only cases, a five-leg Indianapolis emulated-touch/simulated-walking flow in both orientations, and an injured-escort save hold/resume fixture. A forced A/B reproduced a possible identity-replacement mechanism in the old random crime selector while the existing guard prevented it; the original historical disappearance was never traced, so its exact cause remains unknown — what 15.3.1 ships is not a retrod proof but the removal of the identified replacement/streaming-loss mechanisms themselves (verified by pack C110 and the `run1531_*` fixtures). The first hold/resume fixture failed without useful diagnostics; a version positioning the player at the saved witness passed. These checks do not establish universal routing or physical Android behavior.


## Detective follow-up and evidence boundary (J02, 15.6.6)

The scene-evidence and person-of-interest follow-up calls are individually enabled as `J02.SCENE` and `J02.POI`; the parent `J02` career remains OFF and broader career content is not claimed complete. A filed scene record documents only that a vehicle was present at a location. It does not identify an owner or establish guilt. A later arrest-for-investigation option requires a saved, secured witness lead plus a filed, linked scene observation; it remains an investigative disposition, not a guilt finding. A denied statement is recorded as a denial only. A voluntary station handoff is distinct from arrest, and an escaped person may be approached in a later follow-up. Complete filed scene, interview, voluntary-handoff and investigation-only-arrest records earn career credit; an escape, unresolved handoff or withdrawal does not. These outcomes and their linkage survive save/reload; they do not simulate a court finding or broader lasting world consequences.

## Law, radar and civilian safety boundary (15.0.5 scoped checkpoint)

A narrow touch fix for the radar HUD button resolves a short touch on release and suppresses the matching synthesized click, while leaving mouse/keyboard activation and canceled or dragged touches distinct. This is not a general HUD redesign or physical-device sign-off.

The working cruiser radar requires a stopped, working police vehicle on a mapped road; its narrow beam and same-road/visibility checks can miss vehicles. Since 15.3.2 an over-limit reading from an open patrol shift is grounds to signal a traffic stop: one stable branch hash per (world, car) decides shoulder halt, junction-then-stop or a refusal that is honored — a refusal is a refusal, not a chase. The officer parks, exits and walks up; the stopped driver is a durable, mortal person under the 15.3.1 identity system who can be asked for papers, warned, ticketed or searched. A search needs grounds the law would recognize, a clean search and calm papers never produce an arrest, custody is walkable only to the station doors where a booking is recorded — inside the building when the night lets it, honestly doors-recorded when it does not — and a severe reading keeps the car curbed until a unit tows it. The fine leaves the driver's own wallet; a bolter is logged as evaded and nobody is compelled. Since 15.3.3 the call is answered: “call a unit” from a live escort stop dispatches one driven cruiser per shift onto the traffic network — spawned through the engine's own view and oncoming checks, routed around congestion with a jam blacklist and hard caps (a unit that cannot arrive says so honestly), holding at the curb for 70 s for the arrest to book — and a severe booking hands the prisoner to that unit, which hooks its own car and hauls it to the station doors. The haul is pinned, not physically simulated; every bail-out hands the car back to dispatch's own collection timer, and going off duty releases the unit with the evidence still attached. What remains unsimulated: multiple units or radio-priority tactics, pursuit driving toward a refusing car (a refusal stays a refusal), and impound lots beyond the doors. Physical-device behavior and every road or pursuit remain unverified.

An installed-source civilian-reaction check (H45) failed once without a projectile hit; subsequent instrumented runs passed, but the original cause remains unconfirmed. The scoped 15.0.5 checkpoint is locked with that historical uncertainty explicitly retained. Controlled A/B tests demonstrate that a slow simulation can exceed the fixture’s fixed 90-step wait and a vehicle can intercept the projectile, but the original result did not capture enough information to identify its cause. The QA fixture now records relevant projectile, timing and vehicle details without changing its pass criterion. A later QA-only adjustment measures waiting in simulation time, rejects already-occupied live-vehicle lanes, and temporarily disables ambient traffic generation for the direct-hit fixture; it restores the user setting and retains the hit, heat and flee assertions. A vehicle inserted after selection remains a recorded failure, and a separate real-map traffic fixture checks projectile interception. A newer failed run captured vehicle interception after lane selection; the older failure is still unexplained.

The opt-in Auto-fire Safety Assist covers supported automatic straight shots toward known civilians, not blasts, all attack types, or unseen people. Civilian injury consequences, civilian warning and squad fire discipline are independent options. Tested direct ballistic/straight-spell evidence, delayed bleeding sources and projectile launch coordinates survive relevant rewind paths. Known non-player attacks do not create player heat in the tested paths, but legacy/unconverted causes and actor-specific command responsibility remain limitations. Durable actor identity within a session shipped at 15.3.1, and player-operated traffic stops — lights, lawful signals, walk-up contact, tickets, custodial escort, booking and tow orders — shipped at 15.3.2; the arriving dispatch unit, the booking hand-off and the unit-driven tow shipped at 15.3.3; cross-case witness memory and linked accountability now ship in J02 at 15.6.6; broader T19 consequences, pursuit escalation and multi-unit tactics remain deferred.

## Player-car squad boundary (historical 15.0.6 scoped checkpoint; current profile updated at 15.1.4)

**Historical scope note:** This describes the 15.0.6 checkpoint (2026-11-04), when overflow convoy was disabled. T22 was re-enabled at 15.1.4-LOCKED; the 15.0.6 state is not the current profile.

Historical 15.0.6 behavior (2026-11-04): with overflow convoy disabled, eligible squad members could board the player's vehicle; those without a compatible seat held position with a map marker. Horn and player re-entry refreshed the shared hold marker; a nearby on-foot return or a newly available seat released the affected members. These were sampled behaviors, not a guarantee of every terrain, combat or pathing situation. Player-entry overlap safeguards were checked for tractor hay and army-truck bus stops; if relocation could not be safely found, the prop remained. Warehouse forklift placement preferred an open strip but could retain a fallback when none existed. At that 15.0.6 checkpoint, AI overflow convoy and herringbone were deferred, not fixed; current enablement is recorded below.

Current T21 behavior, shipped since 15.4.1–15.4.3: Since 15.4.1 the fireteam following on foot has curb sense against live traffic: it will not step into a moving band unless a gap in the engine's own swept vehicle footprints opens, waits at the curb for at most six seconds before crossing regardless (a follower never permanently refuses the roadway), retargets a formation slot parked mid-lane to the curb beside it, and takes exactly one bounded dash out if geometry pins it in the band. Chase and flank moves bypass all of it — curb sense never pauses a firefight — and a world without live traffic pays one null check. Since 15.4.2 the same fireteam folds its wedge into a file through tight walled alleys: whole-team compression off the roadway, a lone blocked slot dropping just its member to a file rank or a curb-shifted variant, reform two seconds after the corridor clears, and formation changes that only ever move targets — members never teleport or overlap to make one. Since 15.4.3 a squad member facing a long diagonal wade into a live band walks its own curb strip instead and crosses once, short and square, beside the leader — a detour is taken only when the engine's own sampling promises at least a third of the roadway time saved and no more than 45% extra walking, otherwise it goes straight and the curb rules above govern every step; formation members queueing through an alley skip the detour logic entirely, and curb-side formation slots step off walls when the lip hugs them. Roadway tightness is handled by the curb rules above rather than the alley rule, and companions plus non-follow orders keep their previous behavior. This is caution, not invulnerability: cars can still run squad members over exactly as before, no crosswalk or signal is consulted, and no pedestrian right-of-way is simulated.

## Save, backup and map-scale boundary (15.0.7 scoped checkpoint)

If stored progress or options cannot be read, saving that data is blocked and the warning offers a raw-data download when bytes are available. Failed storage writes are reported and can be retried after storage is available. Explicit progress backup exports JSON; importing replaces progress after confirmation, retains one prior local raw progress value, and leaves options and active-run state out. Restoring the pre-import copy replaces current progress. Download a separate backup first, close other game tabs, and do not clear site data while a warning is unresolved. These mechanisms cannot guarantee recovery from device loss, browser eviction or concurrent writes and do not constitute cloud backup or an atomic multi-key transaction.

Real-map DEFAULT is the internal half-scale baseline, with relative ±25% options. Existing 1:3, 1:2 and full-size world/drop identities remain distinct; labels do not rename old keys. These are sampled browser checks, not a procedural-world coordinate migration or comprehensive legacy-save/device verification. Generated-world road and building rescaling stays deferred.
